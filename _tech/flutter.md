  - q: "What is the difference between a StatelessWidget and a StatefulWidget?"
    a: |
      A `StatelessWidget` is rebuilt purely from its constructor arguments. A
      `StatefulWidget` owns a `State` object that persists across rebuilds and calls
      `setState()` to trigger a rebuild when its data changes.
