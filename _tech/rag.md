---
title: RAG
category: ai
summary: Retrieval-Augmented Generation, grounding LLM answers in your own documents.
questions:
  - q: "What is Retrieval-Augmented Generation?"
    a: |
      Instead of relying only on what the model memorised, you retrieve the most relevant
      chunks of your own documents (usually by vector similarity) and pass them to the LLM
      as context, so answers are grounded in that data and can cite it.
